Secondary Missions (Side Hustles)
•	ORANGE mission widgets located at your base. 
•	There is a 5-minute cooldown per badge per box before that person can scan into that widget.
•	Uses orange box widgets
o	Missions can be run in parallel on multiple widgets
•	System randomly selects 4 Points of Interest (POI)
•	Players use orange mission widgets to scan the RESOURCE they want at the POI
•	Players must secure and scan as many supply resources as possible
•	More locations = higher difficulty = better rewards
•	Mission ends when the Mission Completion tag is scanned at base OR when the 45-minute timer expires
